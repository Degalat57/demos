- Card grid: 1 column at 360px, 2 at 768px, 3 at 1280px
- Self-checks: well-formed HTML, viewport meta, column count per breakpoint, CSS brace balance
- Checked in headless Chromium at 360, 768 and 1280px with no horizontal overflow
